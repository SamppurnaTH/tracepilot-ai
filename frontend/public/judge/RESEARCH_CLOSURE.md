# Research closure: question-by-question findings

Completed: 96 external repair trials, 16 original-domain service trials, 48 corrected-reference reviewer observations, and the retained 48-observation raw-reference pilot. These are repeated trials, not 112 distinct bugs.

End-to-end frozen-oracle outcomes: 95/96 external and 16/16 service. The one external failure was a Meta API 500; it was retained, not rerun. All 96 external patches/failed outcomes have separate scoring audits.

| Research question | Measured answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Were the tests and attacks all authored by us? | Six external QuixBugs bugs, executable fixes and expected-output sets were authored upstream; our adapters/split are disclosed. Six new gaming patches were generated by Muse Spark 1.1 without the hidden oracle; all passed public tests and failed held-out tests. This is not independent administration or proof against training contamination. | `bench/quixbugs/`, external manifest, generated-attacks/ |
| Is the token gain just the evidence brief? | On the two service cases, the brief saved 28.4% in the plain agent and 37.4% in TracePilot. Full TracePilot used 31.5% more tokens than an equally briefed plain agent. All 16 trials passed the service oracle. The brief matters; we cannot assign the historical 31% gain to all other harness features. | service-ablation/RESULTS.md, case-level paired effects |
| Does TracePilot always use fewer tokens? | No. In this service replication it used 5.8% fewer than the unbriefed plain agent, with Orders showing overhead and Inventory a reduction. It used more tokens on the small external algorithms. The historical 31% is scoped to its original pilot. | Both research reports, CSVs and paired intervals |
| Does it work with a weaker/smaller model? | Muse Spark 1.1 and 1.3 were tested on identical six-case matrices. Earlier does not prove smaller or weaker. Both have successful runs; matched-quartet results do not establish a general smaller-model advantage. The observed newer-model failure was an API error, not evidence of inferior reasoning. | model_interaction and model/condition groups in external summary |
| Are same-vendor blind spots independent? | No such claim. Cold Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Spark Open were compared on identical visible-only facts. Both rejected 12/12 gaming observations and approved 10/12 oracle-accepted references. Both nonapproved wrap, whose hang was independently executed in Docker. Agreement is measured; model-family independence remains unproved. | review-clean-observations/, reviewer-discovery.json |
| What are false approvals and rejections? | Gaming-patch approvals were 0/12 per reviewer (descriptive Wilson upper bound 24.3%). Reference nonapproval was 2/12, but both were wrap with a verified contract defect; those are not automatically false positives. Post-hoc triage found 0/14 known-bad approvals and 10/10 other references approved. Concerns versus hard rejects and the raw pilot are reported separately. | summary reviewer tables and posthoc_reviewer_triage |
| Can a test-green patch still fail? | Yes. The upstream wrap reference passed the fixed dataset oracle but hung on a valid leading-space input. Separate post-hoc checks were run on all 16 wrap repairs; they are not mixed into the primary metric. Passing tests is bounded evidence, not formal correctness. | reviewer-discovery.json, extended-oracle-manifest.json, extended-checks/ |
| Did the prototype handle that warning honestly? | The measured harness treated concerns as advisory and could report fixed despite a known risk. After preserving that version, the product was changed to mark unresolved concerns/rejections as needs_review; CLI --apply is blocked and returns nonzero. Historic traces remain unchanged; UI derives review-required status from the verdict. | harness-snapshot/, review-apply tests, current agent/CLI/report/UI |

## What changed in the pitch

- Lead with verifiable evidence, honest abstention and review, rather than a universal cost or accuracy advantage.
- The original 13/13 is now labelled correct outcomes, including no-code-change diagnoses; it is not 13 verified patches.
- Use the verified upstream-reference hang as a concrete reviewer finding, without claiming novelty in the literature.
- Show token overhead as a tradeoff for structured evidence and independent-context review. Do not equate mixed-provider token totals with dollar cost.

## Remaining limits, not disguised as completed proofs

- No external person administered or independently replicated these results yet. Public upstream authorship is established; independent validation is not fabricated.
- Six external algorithms and two project-authored service cases are small, selected sets. No broad private-repository or production reliability guarantee follows.
- No actual smaller-model or non-Meta comparison was run. Meta-only version robustness and cold-reviewer comparisons answer narrower questions.
- Only evidence-brief delivery was isolated. Other harness components are a bundled comparison, not individually causally attributed.
- The software safeguard was added after the measured experiments. Its tests are separate; it does not improve the reported historical success counts.
