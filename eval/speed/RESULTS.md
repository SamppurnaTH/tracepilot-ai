# Speed experiment

Generated by `backend/scripts/speed_experiment.py` from `observations.jsonl`. Variants were interleaved round-robin on the same scenarios, in the Docker sandbox. Small samples: medians, not significance tests.

Tests verifying a fix is not enough: a patch can pass every test and still be wrong, which is what the independent reviewer checks. A speed-up counts only if reviewer approvals hold too.

| Variant | What changes | Runs | Tests verified | Reviewer approved | Median time | Median model wait | Median tokens | Median model calls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | Current behaviour | 8 | 8/8 | 8/8 | 175 s | 154 s | 96,126 | 13 |
| no_self_test | Fix phase: the model is told the harness runs the tests, so it needn't run them itself | 8 | 8/8 | 6/8 | 148 s | 130 s | 65,641 | 10 |
| low_effort | Reasoning effort set to low | 8 | 8/8 | 7/8 | 149 s | 127 s | 74,384 | 11 |

## Per scenario (median seconds)

| Scenario | baseline | no_self_test | low_effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| orders | 146 (2) | 136 (2) | 140 (2) |
| notes | 175 (2) | 140 (2) | 143 (2) |
| billing | 191 (2) | 141 (2) | 141 (2) |
| inventory | 279 (2) | 221 (2) | 224 (2) |

## Runs not verified or not approved by the reviewer

- no_self_test / billing #1: needs_review, reviewer concerns
- low_effort / billing #1: needs_review, reviewer concerns
- no_self_test / billing #2: needs_review, reviewer concerns

## Decision

Keep the current behaviour. Both speed-ups cut the median time by about 15%, but cost reviewer approvals: on Billing, whose hidden trap (an empty invoice) no test covers, the patches drew concerns in 2 of 2 no-self-test runs and 1 of 2 low-effort runs, against 0 of 2 at baseline. Eight runs per variant is a small sample, but TracePilot's promise is proof beyond green tests, so speed doesn't buy back a weaker fix.
